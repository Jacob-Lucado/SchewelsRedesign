Furniture you can pay for the way your neighbors always have.

In 1897 Elias Schewel started selling chairs door to door in Lynchburg and let customers pay a little at a time. Five generations on, we still hold our own accounts, so a new sofa doesn’t have to wait for payday.

One family, one idea, 129 years.

We’ve grown from a wagon route into one of the oldest family-run furniture retailers in the country.

  1. 1897

    Elias Schewel sells chairs door to door in Lynchburg.

  2. 1917

    The business incorporates as Schewel Furniture Company.

  3. 1941

    The Luray store opens, our start in Page County.

  4. 2019

    We become Schewels Home to cover the whole house.

  5. 2022

    125th anniversary, celebrated in all of our stores.

  6. 2025

    Central Furniture and Butterworth’s join the family.
