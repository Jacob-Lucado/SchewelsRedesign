Questions we hear most

Who holds my account?

We do. Schewels carries its own accounts, so you deal with the same people who sold you the furniture.

What if I don't qualify for an account?

We also offer lease-to-own options. Ask your showroom which one fits.

How do I make a payment?

Use "Make a payment" at the top of any page, or stop by your showroom.
