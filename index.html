Three ways to rebuild the Schewels Home homepage.

Each concept is a working page you can click through at desktop and phone width. They share the same real content (rooms, financing, 180-night trial, 52 showrooms, the 1897 story), use the brand’s red, black, and white with Montserrat, and take different bets on what the homepage should lead with.

What the current homepage gets wrong

The best story is at the bottom.Own-account financing and 125+ years of family history are what set Schewels apart, and both sit below the fold under a promo slider.
Twelve top-level menu items, 100+ links.Lawn & Garden sits beside Living Room. “Benches” shows up in four menus and “Accent Chairs” in three. The menu markup is also repeated twice in the page.
Ads are baked into images.Dozens of slider and promo graphics carry their text inside the picture, with alt text like “SAVE”, “SAE”, and “DAED”. Screen readers get nothing and the text can’t reflow on phones.
Utility links live in the category row.“Make a Payment” and “Financing” are mixed in with “Sale” and the departments, so existing customers hunt for them.
The store picker starts empty.The location modal opens with “Select a Showroom” and a “0 stores” message before anyone has typed anything.
Three promo systems at once.A hero slider, a deals grid, and a popup all compete for the same attention, plus a chat widget. Nothing is clearly first.

Based on the page content as published today. The live site blocked my automated browser, so I couldn’t capture how it renders visually. Worth a quick eyeball pass against these points.

The concepts

A. Since 1897

Leads with what no big-box store can say: they’ve let neighbors pay over time for 129 years.

  • Story-first hero with a six-point timeline
  • Financing explained in plain terms, mid-page
  • Custom upholstery fabric picker
Open concept

B. Room first

Treats the homepage as a shopping tool. Pick a room and the hero changes, with search and department links always in reach.

  • Interactive room picker in the hero
  • Monthly payment estimator
  • Pay and sign-in separated from shopping
Open concept

C. Find your Schewels

Puts the store locator where the slider is. With 52 showrooms and in-person financing, the local store is the product.

  • Live-filtered list of all 52 showrooms with real store links
  • Directions from each store card
  • Financing framed as a conversation
Open concept

They also combine well.

B’s header and room picker with A’s financing section and timeline would cover both browsers and loyal customers. C’s store finder works as a standalone page or a header dropdown on either.

Unofficial redesign concepts made for review. Photos are stand-ins, and the payment estimator is demo math. Nothing here replaces the live site’s prices or terms.
