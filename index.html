Every demo actually works

One small shared script powers the shopping features, so each concept can keep its own look.

1Cart and saved items

Slide-out drawer with quantities, a saved tab, and a monthly estimate from 12 to 60 months.

2Quick view

Open any product for details, add it, or save it, without leaving the page.

3Search suggestions

Type a few letters and get matching products and rooms. Arrow keys and Enter work.

4Showroom picker

All 52 showrooms, searchable by name or state, with links to the real store pages.

5Filter and sort

Room chips and a sort menu on every product grid.

6Black Friday savings

In D, the cart applies the $50 to $200 tiers as the total climbs, with a progress bar.

7Remembers you

Cart, favorites and showroom stay put across concepts and reloads.

8Phone ready

Every page is built to fit a phone with no sideways scrolling.

Deploy and share

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Nothing to build.

Put it online

  • GitHub Pages: Settings, Pages, deploy from main and / (root).
  • Netlify, Vercel or Cloudflare Pages: point at the repo, leave the build command empty.
  • Links are relative, so it works at a sub-path like /SchewelsRedesign/.
  • Pages are marked noindex, and robots.txt asks crawlers to stay out.

Know what's sample

  • Photos are stand-ins. Swap in Schewels photography before any real use.
  • Products and prices are made up for the demo.
  • Payment numbers are total divided by months, not a real finance quote.
  • Cart and reserve buttons don't take orders or money.
